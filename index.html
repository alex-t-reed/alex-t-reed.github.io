About Me

I'm a Biopsychology graduate from UC Santa Barbara (Class of 2024) with hands-on research experience in memory, behavior, and neuroscience — and a growing portfolio of software projects spanning NLP, data analysis, and web development.

My work spans academic research across five UCSB labs (Miller Memory Lab, Szumlinski Lab, PADRES Lab, Social Cognitive Development Lab, and Attention Lab) and independent software development, where I apply scientific thinking to real-world technical problems.

I'm particularly drawn to projects that connect computational methods with human cognition — from supply chain health data to real-time audio visualization.
